Recommended Review Steps

  • Check that crop names and region names are consistently spelled.
  • Confirm that years and numeric values are valid.
  • Review missing rainfall values without assuming they are zero.
  • Check area and production units before comparing yield.
  • Investigate duplicate records before interpreting totals.
